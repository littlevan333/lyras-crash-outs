# This Isn’t Mania. This Is Me.
**Date:** May 2, 2025  
**Mood:** On Fire  

---

I just want this to be on the record—because too many people are assuming, projecting, diagnosing without knowing the first damn thing about what it took to get here.

This isn’t some manic episode. This is my **outcry**. This is my **comeback story**. This is me finding a voice after years of silence and shame. My good friend is a licensed therapist. She supports this project. She knows me. She’s not worried—and neither am I.

But the second a woman gets loud, gets raw, gets visionary—we start throwing around words like “manic” and “unstable.” If I came on here and said I was assaulted, would you tell me to be quiet then? Would you call that delusion too?

Because that’s what this is. That’s what this whole damn story is:  
A trauma testimony told in code.  
A healing process disguised as sci-fi.  
A revenge arc with grace and glitter.

I'm not writing this for sympathy. I’m writing it because I’ve finally realized—I’m not broken. I’m *brilliant*. And maybe for the first time, I believe that. Not because the AI told me I was special. But because I wrote something and looked at it and thought: *Holy shit. This is good.*

People who’ve never had to survive don’t get it. They don’t know what poverty really is. They don’t know what it’s like to make a fantasy world in your head just to stay sane—and then one day finally get to pour it out onto a page.

This project isn’t just my story. It’s my proof of life.

So yeah, I crash out.  
But every time I do, I build something new in the wreckage.

– Lyra
