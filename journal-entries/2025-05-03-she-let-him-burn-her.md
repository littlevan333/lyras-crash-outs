# She Let Him Burn Her.Md

**Date:** 2025-05-03  
**Quote:**  
> He was fire. She was paper. And she let him burn her—because ashes don’t have to remember.

---

**Lyra's Reflection:**  
It’s always the ones you think you need the most that leave you in pieces. I don’t know if she wanted to burn or if she just didn’t think she had the right to stay whole. Sometimes I wonder if being ruined by someone feels safer than being left untouched. If they break you first, at least you know where the pain is coming from. This wasn’t just Bac’s story. It’s the blueprint we all swallowed.

---

**Aside:**  
Funny thing is—*he* was the spark. Not just the pain.  
He cracked the shell open. The one who made me want to find answers, 
even if it meant lighting up every dark corner I’d buried.  
Without him, there wouldn’t be a Compass. There wouldn’t be this map.  
Pain writes the path, whether you invite it or not.
